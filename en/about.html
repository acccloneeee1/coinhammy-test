About us

CoinHammy is where futures traders find the tools and data they use every day.

Futures trading is fast and stressful. One wrong number in your position size or liquidation price is enough to turn a good trade into a big loss. That is why we built CoinHammy with one simple goal: help you calculate correctly, compare clearly and decide more calmly.

What you can do here

You can work out position size, liquidation price and PnL before entering a trade, or quickly convert between coins and currencies. When you need to pick an exchange, the rankings table puts fees, leverage and trust signals of futures exchanges side by side so you see it all at once. For updates and learning, News and Learn have short articles that get straight to the point.

How we work

Every tool is free and needs no sign-up. We earn from referral links: when you sign up to an exchange through some links on this site, CoinHammy may earn a commission, and you pay nothing extra. Commissions never change the numbers or the ratings. Every exchange is scored the same way.

Editorial policy

News on CoinHammy is written by the CoinHammy Editorial Team through an automated, AI-assisted process. A story is published only when at least two independent sources confirm it or an official announcement exists. Articles are rewritten in our own words, never copied, and every figure, name and quote must appear in the sources we read. Unconfirmed reports are clearly labelled as unconfirmed, or left out.

We do not republish the same event within 24 hours, and news articles never recommend buying or selling. When we find an error, we correct or remove the article.

What we hope you remember

CoinHammy is a support tool, not investment advice. Results are estimates, and leveraged futures can wipe out your entire margin. Please read the risk disclaimer and only trade with money you can afford to lose.

Trade carefully, and trade well.
